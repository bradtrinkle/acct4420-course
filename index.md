ACCT/MIS 4420 and 5420 · Spring 2027 · Online, asynchronous

**Your goal for this course:** when you face a task, your first thought is *"How can AI help me do this?"* and your second is *"How do I know the answer is right?"*

## The story

You are a new hire at **Velox Motorworks**, a publicly traded maker of high-performance sports cars based in Des Moines, Iowa. Velox's CFO, Priya Natarajan, has launched an AI-first finance initiative, and your class is the first hire class in the new finance rotational program.

| Weeks | Where you are | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | Tallgrass Advisory training | Learn to work and communicate with AI |
| 4 to 7 | Velox rotations | Apply AI in Financial Accounting, Management Accounting, Auditing, and Taxation |
| 8 and 9 | Velox, with Tallgrass | Build AI workflows and agents with human oversight |
| 10 to 12 | A Tallgrass client | Design, test, and demonstrate your own AI solution |

Your guide throughout is **Jordan Reyes**, an AI trainer at Tallgrass Advisory. Jordan is a fictional character, and the videos use an AI avatar.

## How every module works

1. Mission brief
2. Try it first
3. Learn
4. Ethics checkpoint
5. First attempt, then feedback from your Jordan coach
6. Mastery attempt (the version that is graded)
7. Quiz
8. Reflection

Plan for about 7 hours a week. The final project weeks are heavier.
