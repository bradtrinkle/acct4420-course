# Module 2 rubric (100 points)

Your mastery attempt is scored against this rubric. Every level describes something visible in your submission. Your Jordan coach uses the same rubric.

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Structured prompt and process (25) | The Round 2 request contains all five parts (role, goal, audience, format, constraints), a grounding statement in the student's own words (relies only on the attached data and says what to do when the data is silent), an instruction about the example (what to copy and not copy: style yes, numbers no), and a line asking Claude to ask questions first. Claude's questions and your answers are shown. (5 points per element, 25 total) | Some elements present; award 5 for each one present (5 to 20) | No structured request shown (0) |
| B. Verification (15) | Three specific claims, each with where you looked, a verdict that is correct when checked against the data, and what you did about it (15) | Three claims, but one or more lacks a location, a correct verdict, or an action (7) | Fewer than three claims, or claims not checked against the data (0) |
| C. Ethical AI: confidentiality (20) | All eight notes sorted Keep, Generalize, or Remove with a reason; at least six decisions are reasonable and explained; the safe-file method is described and no confidential note remains in the file or prompt (20) | Sorted with reasons, but fewer than six reasonable, or the method is unclear (10) | A confidential note was used with AI, or no decisions shown (0) |
| D. Comparison and Playbook (20) | At least two specific differences between Round 1 and Round 2 tied to parts of the request (7); a reusable template with [BRACKETS] and the five parts, grounding statement, example instruction, and questions-first line (7); a note to Walt of 150 words or fewer covering why he can trust the method, the check step, and what stays out of the prompt (6) | Award the points for each part that is present | Missing (0) |
| E. AI use disclosure (5) | Lists the tools used and what each did (5) | Tools listed without saying what they did (2) | Missing (0) |
| F. Mastery evidence (15) | Coach transcript included, and the revision visibly addresses its main points or explains why feedback was rejected (15) | Transcript included, but no visible revision (7) | No transcript (0) |

**Graduate students (5420):** criterion G adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit means two questions the data cannot answer, each asked with and without a grounding instruction, with the answers compared and the difference explained (10); partial means one comparison or no explanation (5).

You are scored on your reasoning, not on matching an answer key. A different decision on a confidentiality note, with a sound reason, earns credit.
