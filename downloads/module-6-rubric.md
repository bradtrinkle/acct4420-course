**Graduate students (5420):** criterion H adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit: a third verified finding with the files, your steps, and the result (3); a check that does not use AI, such as rebuilding the whole first-digit test in a spreadsheet and comparing it with Claude's table (4); at least one assumption or limitation that affects your recommendation, with how it could change what the investigation owner should do (3). Partial credit is awarded for each part present.

You are scored on your reasoning and evidence, not on matching wording. Your own words for each request are fine.
