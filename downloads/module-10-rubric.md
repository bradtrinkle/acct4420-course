# Module 10 rubric (100 points): The Final Project

Your final package is scored against this rubric once, at the end of Week 12. Every level describes something visible in your submission. Your Jordan coach uses the same rubric to give you feedback in Weeks 10 and 11, but the coach does not grade. The table covers the same criteria shown at the bottom of Step 6 of the Module 10 page, in more detail. Upload this file to your Jordan Coach project, not to Canvas.

**Graduate students (5420):** criterion L adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit: a third finding from your November or December report, checked with an independent source or a calculation done a different way from the first two, with the steps, the result, and at least one explained assumption or limitation (3); a second build type tried on October, with its scorer result and a comparison of setup effort, risk, and checking effort, and a reasoned recommendation (4); a monitoring plan for a year, naming what to measure monthly, what would trigger a re-test, who decides, and one way the solution could slowly get worse without anyone noticing and how you would catch it (3). Partial credit is awarded for each part present.

Only your first scored run of November and your first scored run of December count, and the report you paste must be the one from that run. How the scorer works: it reads the lines of the report in the required format, compares what they say with the facts in that month's case files, and checks how the report handled each of the four traps. It logs every run, and your submission includes the log. Your grader recomputes the same numbers from the report you pasted.

You are scored on your reasoning and evidence, not on matching wording. Your own words for each request are fine.
