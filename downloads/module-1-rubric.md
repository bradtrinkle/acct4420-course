# Module 1 rubric (100 points)



Your mastery attempt is scored against this rubric. Every level describes something visible in your submission. Your Jordan coach uses the same rubric.

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Task sort (20) | All 10 tasks placed in a category, each with a reason naming a specific AI strength or risk (20) | All placed, but 3 or more reasons are generic, such as "AI is good at this" (10) | Fewer than 10 tasks placed (0) |
| B. AI testing (25) | Three tests, including task 10, each with the prompt, an output summary, a named verification method, and a result (25) | Three tests, but one or more lacks a verification method or result (12) | Fewer than three tests, or task 10 not tested (0) |
| C. Ethical AI, task 10 (20) | Shows the data was anonymized before use, with the method described, and names confidentiality plus at least one other policy area with a reason (20) | Anonymized, but the method is unclear or only one policy area is named (10) | Raw personal data was used, or no anonymization is shown (0) |
| D. Memo to the CFO (15) | 350 words or fewer, addressed to Priya Natarajan, with at least two specific recommendations tied to the sort (15) | Over 350 words, or recommendations not tied to the sort (7) | Missing (0) |
| E. AI use disclosure (5) | Lists the tools used and what each did (5) | Tools listed without saying what they did (2) | Missing (0) |
| F. Mastery evidence (15) | Coach transcript included, and the revision visibly addresses its main points or explains why feedback was rejected (15) | Transcript included, but no visible revision (7) | No transcript (0) |

**Graduate students (5420):** criterion G adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit means two prompts tested on each of two tasks, with the outputs compared and a reason why one worked better (10); partial means one comparison or no reason (5).

You are scored on your reasoning, not on matching an answer key. A different category with a sound reason earns full credit.
