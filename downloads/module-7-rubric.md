**Graduate students (5420):** criterion H adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit: a third verified finding with the files, your steps, and the result (3); a check that does not use AI and uses at least one source outside the packet (for example, reading IRS Notice 2026-11 or section 168 at the web address at the end of the packet and comparing it with notes S3 and S4), or a rebuild of the whole calculation in a spreadsheet compared with Claude's (4); at least one assumption or limitation that affects your recommendation, with how it could change what Grace should do (3). Partial credit is awarded for each part present.

You are scored on your reasoning and evidence, not on matching wording. Your own words for each request are fine.
