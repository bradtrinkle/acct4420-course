# Velox Responsible AI Policy



Every Velox employee who uses AI follows these five rules. They are consistent with the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct principles of integrity, objectivity, due care, and confidentiality.

| Area | The rule | What it looks like at Velox |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Confidentiality and privacy | Never put confidential, personal, or nonpublic data into an AI tool unless it is approved for that data, and anonymize first | Remove names, employee IDs, and salaries before analysis |
| 2. Accuracy | Treat AI output as a draft. Verify facts, figures, and sources before you rely on them | Recalculate key numbers; check rates and citations at the source |
| 3. Bias | Ask whether the data or the AI's answer could treat a group unfairly, and test for it | Check that a layoff or credit analysis doesn't hinge on age, gender, or location |
| 4. Transparency and disclosure | Say when and how AI contributed to your work | Note "AI-assisted draft, reviewed by [name]" on deliverables |
| 5. Human accountability | A person owns every decision and every signature. AI never signs off | The preparer and reviewer are named people, not a tool |

In this course, every assignment includes an ethics criterion based on this policy. Course materials use only simulated or public information.
