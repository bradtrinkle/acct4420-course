**Graduate students (5420):** criterion H adds 10 points and the total is scaled back to 100. Full credit: a second scenario you built from a copy of the October files (whose answers you already know), with at least three planted problems and your private answer key, and the machine's memo for it (3); a comparison with November in real numbers (caught, missed, false alarms by hand count) and a reason for any difference (4); at least one explained improvement to the instructions, stages, gates, or tests, with a way to test it that avoids overfitting to your own scenario (3). Partial credit is awarded for each part present.

Only your first scored run of November and your first scored run of December count, and the memo you paste must be the one from that run, matching the stage output you paste. How the scorer works: it reads the codes in your memo (for example DEP9930, L08, P-900123, made-up codes), compares them with hashed answer keys, and counts planted problems caught, false alarms, and codes that are not in the month's files. It logs every run, and your submission includes the log. Your grader recomputes the same numbers from the memo you pasted.

You are scored on your reasoning and evidence, not on matching wording. Your own words for each request are fine.
