Your story: you are a new hire at Velox

For the next 12 weeks you play yourself in a made-up job. Velox Motorworks is a fictional, publicly traded maker of high-performance sports cars. It is headquartered in Des Moines, Iowa, with a second plant in South Carolina. Its CFO, Priya Natarajan, has just launched an AI-first finance program: she wants every person in finance to ask, before starting any task, "How can AI help me do this?" You are in the first hire class.

Velox hired an AI consulting firm, Tallgrass Advisory, to train you. Your guide there is Jordan Reyes. Jordan is a fictional character, and the course videos show Jordan as an AI-generated avatar.

What this means for you each week. A message arrives from someone at Velox with a realistic finance problem and fictional data. You use your own Claude account to work on it, check what the AI gives you, and write up what you found, the way a new analyst would. You are graded on your judgment: how well you use AI, how carefully you check it, and how clearly you explain your results. You do not need to be an accounting expert, and you do not need to know how to program.

How the 12 weeks unfold

  • Weeks 1 to 3, Tallgrass training. You learn what AI can and cannot do and how to ask it for good work, and you build your own AI coach.
  • Weeks 4 to 7, Velox rotations. You spend a week each with the Controller's Office (financial accounting), the South Carolina plant (management accounting), Internal Audit, and the Tax Department.
  • Weeks 8 and 9, building with Tallgrass. You turn one-off tasks into a repeatable workflow, and then into an AI agent that investigates problems with a person in charge.
  • Weeks 10 to 12, final project. You solve a problem for a Tallgrass client and present it to them.

How every module works

1 · Try it first

Use AI on a real Velox task before anyone teaches you.

2 · Think it through

Short lessons with questions you answer. You move on when you have thought it through.

3 · Get feedback

Your own Jordan coach reviews your draft, then you improve it.

4 · Show mastery

Submit your improved version. That is the one that counts.

5 · Stay responsible

Every module practices the Velox Responsible AI Policy.

6 · About 7 hours

A week, at most. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and turn on automatic saving (button at the bottom left).

By the end, when you face a task, your first thought will be "How can AI help me do this?" and your second will be "How do I know the answer is right?"
