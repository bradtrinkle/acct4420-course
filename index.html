How every module works

1 · Try it first

Use AI on a real Velox task before anyone teaches you.

2 · Think it through

Short lessons with questions you answer. You move on when you have thought it through.

3 · Get feedback

Your own Jordan coach reviews your draft, then you improve it.

4 · Show mastery

Submit your improved version. That is the one that counts.

5 · Stay responsible

Every module practices the Velox Responsible AI Policy.

6 · About 7 hours

A week, at most. Your progress saves on this device automatically.

Your goal for this course

By the end, when you face a task, your first thought will be "How can AI help me do this?" and your second will be "How do I know the answer is right?"
