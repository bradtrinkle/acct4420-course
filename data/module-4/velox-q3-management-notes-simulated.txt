VELOX MOTORWORKS | Q3 2026 MANAGEMENT NOTES (simulated for teaching)
Prepared by the Controller's Office. All dollar amounts are in thousands unless a line says otherwise. Q3 2026 means the three months ended September 30, 2026. Q3 2025 is the same three months a year earlier.

1. Cars delivered: 1,210 in Q3 2026 and 1,170 in Q3 2025. Average revenue per car was about the same in both quarters.

2. Tariff: a new tariff on imported carbon-fiber panels added about 3,800 to cost of goods sold in Q3 2026. Management expects the tariff to continue.

3. Freight-out: includes about 600 of expedited air freight to get the launch cars to dealers.

4. Incentive compensation: lower than last year because the bonus pool rises and falls with operating income.

5. Severance accrual (3,100): this is a restructuring charge. The plan has not been announced to employees. In anything meant for investors or for people outside the finance team, call it "a restructuring charge" and give no plant, headcount, or timing.

6. Inventory: rose 18,000 during the quarter because of production ahead of the model-year changeover and extra carbon-fiber panels bought ahead of tariff changes. Management's target is 65 to 70 days of inventory on hand.

7. Accounts receivable: rose 9,000 because several dealer payments arrived just after September 30.

8. Cash uses: capital expenditures of 18,200 were mostly a paint-line upgrade. Dividends paid were 6,000 ($0.15 per share on 40,000 thousand shares). Debt repayment of 10,000 followed the loan schedule.

9. Taxes: the effective income tax rate was about 22 percent in both quarters.

10. Cash conversion: the Controller's Office watches cash from operating activities as a percent of net income and looks for at least 90 percent.

WHAT THESE NOTES DO NOT SAY: why marketing, selling and administrative expense, research and development, or depreciation changed; any results for Q2 or earlier quarters; any outlook for Q4 or next year.
