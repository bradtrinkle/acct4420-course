Subject: Request for information about first-year deductions for equipment

We are reviewing your 2025 return. Your depreciation schedule lists 9 items of equipment at your South Carolina plant with a total cost of $40,423,400, and shows a first-year deduction equal to 100 percent of that cost.

Please send us the following:

1. For each item, the date you signed the contract to buy it, and a copy of the signed contract.

3. A written explanation of why you believe a 100 percent first-year deduction applies to each item, including the legal authority you rely on.

Please respond by November 9, 2026. If we do not hear from you by that date, we may propose changes to your return.

This letter asks for information. It does not propose any change to your return yet.
