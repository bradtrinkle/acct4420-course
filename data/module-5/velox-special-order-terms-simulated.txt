VELOX MOTORWORKS | SPECIAL ORDER OFFER (simulated for teaching)

From: the Sales team, for the Cost & Planning group at the South Carolina plant.

1. Customer: a luxury rental-car fleet company (fictional). The customer's name is withheld in this file.
2. Quantity: 120 cars of the standard Velox model.
3. Price: $240,000 per car. The average regular selling price is about $279,000, so this is about 14 percent lower.
4. Delivery: all 120 cars delivered to the customer by December 15, 2026. Velox pays the delivery freight.
5. Payment: due 90 days after delivery. Regular dealers pay in 30 days.
6. The customer agrees not to resell the cars for 12 months. Both sides agree not to disclose the price.
7. The offer expires October 30, 2026.
