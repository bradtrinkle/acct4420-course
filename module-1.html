Step 3 · Learn · 90 min

3a. What generative AI is

A large language model predicts likely next words from patterns in huge amounts of text. That is why it writes fluently and explains concepts well, and why it can sound confident while being wrong.

Your turn

Your manager says, "AI looked it up, so it's correct." Is that a safe assumption?

3b. Where AI is strong

Drafting, summarizing, explaining, translating, organizing, brainstorming, and a first pass at data you give it. In finance, that is hours back every week.

3c. Where AI needs checking

Large calculations, current facts like tax rates, citations, and anything it cannot see, such as Velox's own records. When AI fills a gap with something made up, that is called a hallucination.

Your turn

Suppose you ask AI for the current Iowa corporate income tax rate. Where would you check its answer?

3d. The three-way sort

Every task gets AI help. The question is how much you check and who owns the decision.

CategoryMeaningExample
AI does it wellLow risk; a quick read is enoughDrafting a routine email
AI helps, a person checksAI does the work; you verify facts and numbersSummarizing contract payment terms
AI assists, a person decidesAI prepares the analysis; a named person makes the judgment or signsRecommending a receivable write-off
