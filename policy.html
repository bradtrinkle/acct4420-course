Velox Responsible AI Policy

Every Velox employee who uses AI follows these five rules. They match the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct principles of integrity, objectivity, due care, and confidentiality. Every assignment in this course includes an ethics check based on this policy.

1 · Confidentiality and privacy

Rule: Never put confidential, personal, or nonpublic data into an AI tool unless it is approved for that data, and anonymize first.

At Velox: remove names, employee IDs, and salaries before analysis.

2 · Accuracy

Rule: Treat AI output as a draft. Verify facts, figures, and sources before you rely on them.

At Velox: recalculate key numbers; check rates and citations at the source.

3 · Bias

Rule: Ask whether the data or the AI's answer could treat a group unfairly, and test for it.

At Velox: check that a layoff or credit analysis doesn't hinge on age, gender, or location.

4 · Transparency and disclosure

Rule: Say when and how AI contributed to your work.

At Velox: note "AI-assisted draft, reviewed by [name]" on deliverables.

5 · Human accountability

Rule: A person owns every decision and every signature. AI never signs off.

At Velox: the preparer and reviewer are named people, not a tool.
