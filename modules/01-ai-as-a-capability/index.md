## Step 2. Try it first (45 min)

> **Jordan:** Before I teach you anything, try something. Open Claude and ask it to do task 5 from the [assignment](assignment.md): explain why Velox's gross margin fell, using the figures provided. Don't overthink your prompt.

**Your turn.** Write down answers to three questions:

1. What did AI do well?
2. What did it get wrong, assume, or leave out?
3. How would you check whether its explanation is right?

Keep these notes. You will compare them to what you know by the end of the week.

## Step 3. Learn (90 min)

Four short segments, each ending in a decision.

**3a. What generative AI is.** A large language model predicts likely next words from patterns in huge amounts of text. That is why it writes fluently and explains concepts well, and why it can sound confident while being wrong.

> **Your turn:** Your manager says, "AI looked it up, so it's correct." Is that a safe assumption? Choose yes or no, and write one sentence why.

**3b. Where AI is strong.** Drafting, summarizing, explaining, translating, organizing, brainstorming, and first-pass analysis of data you provide.

**3c. Where AI needs checking.** Math on large datasets, current facts like tax rates, citations and sources, and anything it cannot see, such as Velox's own records. AI can produce a confident, made-up answer, often called a hallucination.

> **Your turn:** For task 7, finding the current Iowa corporate tax rate, name the source you would use to check AI's answer.

**3d. The three-way sort.** Every task gets AI help. The question is how much you trust the output.

| Category | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI does it well | Low risk; a quick read is enough | Drafting a routine email |
| AI helps, a person checks | AI does the work; you verify facts and numbers | Summarizing contract payment terms |
| AI assists, a person decides | AI prepares the analysis; a named person makes the judgment or signs | Recommending a receivable write-off |

> **Your turn:** Sort this practice task before moving on: "Draft talking points for the CEO's dealer conference." Then check your reasoning against the explanation at the bottom of this page.

## Step 4. Ethics checkpoint (30 min)

Read the [Velox Responsible AI Policy](../../shared/responsible-ai-policy.md).

> **Jordan:** AI champions are the people who use AI the most *and* use it responsibly. The policy is how we do both.

**Your turn.** Answer two scenario questions in your submission template (section C):

1. A coworker pastes the full employee salary file into a public chatbot to save time. Which policy area does this break, and what should they have done?
2. You use AI to draft a variance explanation for the Controller. What does the transparency rule require you to add?

## Step 5. First attempt and coach feedback (90 min)

1. Set up your Jordan coach Project using the [coach setup steps](coach-setup.md).
2. Complete the [core assignment](assignment.md).
3. Paste your full draft into your Jordan coach and ask for feedback.
4. Save the coach's feedback. You will submit it with your final version.

## Step 6. Mastery attempt (60 min)

Revise your work using the coach's feedback. If you disagree with a piece of feedback, keep your version and explain why in one sentence. Spotting bad AI feedback counts as good judgment. Submit the revised assignment and the coach transcript in Canvas.

## Step 7. Quiz (30 min)

Take the Module 1 quiz in Canvas: 10 questions, two attempts, and your higher score counts.

## Step 8. Reflection (15 min)

> **Your turn:** In three to five sentences: think of a task you already do in a job, internship, or another class. How could AI help you do it, and which of the three categories does it fall into?

### Explanation for the practice task in Step 3d

"Draft talking points for the CEO's dealer conference" is **AI does it well, with a quick read.** AI drafts talking points quickly. A person should still read for tone and for any figures or claims about Velox, which AI cannot verify. If the talking points quote financial results, it moves to **AI helps, a person checks**.
