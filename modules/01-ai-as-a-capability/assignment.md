# Core assignment: What Can AI Do for Velox?

> **Jordan:** Priya wants to know where AI fits in Velox finance before the rotations start. Here are 10 real tasks from the finance teams. Sort them, test a few, and tell her what you found.

## What to do

1. Sort all 10 tasks into the three categories, with a one- or two-sentence reason for each.
2. Test AI on **task 10** plus **two other tasks you choose**. For each test, record your prompt, summarize the output, and explain what you checked and how.
3. For task 10, show how you anonymized the data before using AI, and name the policy areas involved.
4. Write a memo to Priya Natarajan, one page maximum, recommending where Velox finance should start using AI.

The three categories: **AI does it well**, **AI helps, a person checks**, and **AI assists, a person decides**. See Step 3d of the [module page](index.md).

## Submission template

Use the Word template in Canvas. It has these fixed sections:

- **A. Task sort:** a table with task number, category, and reason
- **B. AI tests:** for each of three tests, the prompt, an output summary, what you checked, how you checked it, and the result
- **C. Task 10 ethics:** how you anonymized the data and which policy areas apply, plus your answers to the two Step 4 scenario questions
- **D. Memo to Priya Natarajan:** one page maximum
- **E. AI use disclosure:** which tools you used and for what
- **F. Coach feedback:** the full Jordan coach transcript, plus any feedback you rejected and why
