# Set up your Jordan coach

You build this once, in your own Claude account, and reuse it all term. It takes about 10 minutes.

> The steps below describe Claude's website as of this course's writing. If a button is named slightly differently, look for the closest match. Tell your instructor if you get stuck.

## Before you start

You need a paid Claude account. Download these files from this site:

- [Jordan coach instructions](../../shared/jordan-coach-instructions.txt) (open it and keep it handy)
- [Velox Responsible AI Policy](../../shared/responsible-ai-policy.md)
- [Module 1 rubric](rubric.md)

Tip: to save a page as a file, open it, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac), and choose **Save as PDF**.

## Steps

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Projects**.
3. Click **Create project** (it may show as **+ New project**).
4. Name it **Jordan Coach - ACCT 4420**. For the description, type "Feedback coach for my Velox assignments." Click **Create project**.
5. On the project page, find **Instructions** in the right-hand panel and click to edit it (it may say **Set project instructions**).
6. Open the Jordan coach instructions file, copy all of the text, paste it into the box, and click **Save**.
7. In the same panel, find **Project knowledge** (or **Files**), click **+** or **Add content**, and upload the Responsible AI Policy and the Module 1 rubric as PDFs.
8. Start a new chat inside the project and type: "Hi Jordan, I'm ready for Module 1." Jordan should greet you and ask for your draft.

**If Jordan doesn't greet you and ask for your draft,** go back to step 6 and check that your instructions saved.

## Using your coach

- Paste your full draft and ask: "Please give me feedback on this against the rubric."
- Jordan will not write or fix your work. That is on purpose.
- Save the whole conversation. You submit it with your final version.
- Each new module, add that module's rubric to the same project. You do not need to set it up again.

## Privacy reminder

Use only the simulated data in this course. Never put real personal, employer, or client information into your coach.
